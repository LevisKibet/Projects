Rivergence sea monster - the head only (replaces the old sea snake)

Units: 1 unit = 1 Minecraft block. Snout at the origin facing north (-Z); the back of the head is at z = 26,
so it's about 26 blocks long and 15 wide. Blender: import the .obj as is. Blockbench: Generic Model, Import > OBJ,
scale x16 if it comes in tiny.

Parts (keep the names - the game moves them by name):
  skull_top / skull_under / skull_caps   the head
  jaw_top / jaw_under / jaw_caps         the lower jaw (can open a little)
  teeth, nostrils
  eye_left / eye_right                   the glowing almond eyes (they open, close and look at you)
  eye_left_pupil / eye_right_pupil       vertical slit pupils
  brow_left / brow_right                 heavy brows over the eyes
  horn_left / horn_right (+ _small), ridge

In game it sits at the edge of the fog, nearly lost in the dark; when you look at it the eyes open and watch you,
then the head draws back into the murk and is gone. The eye material glows; the hide is very dark violet.
