Rivergence sea life - editable models (rest pose)

Units: 1 unit = 1 Minecraft block. Blender: File > Import > Wavefront (.obj) and keep the scale.
Blockbench: use a "Generic Model", File > Import > OBJ; if it comes in tiny, scale it up 16x (Blockbench counts 16 px per block).
Colours are in the .mtl files (one material per part). Keep the part (object) names if you can - the game animates them by name.

leviathan.obj  head at the origin facing north (-Z), body runs back along +Z, 90 blocks long.
  body_back / body_belly / body_caps : the hide (back darker than the belly)
  spines                             : ridge of fins down the back
  eye_left / eye_right (+ _pupil)    : the glowing eyes and slit pupils (they open/close in game)
  In game the spine bends to follow its path; the head lifts and turns to watch you.

tuna.obj  nose toward +X, 2.4 blocks long, centred on the origin.
  body_back / body_belly, tail (the sickle tail lobes), dorsal_fin, finlets (yellow)
  In game the back half and tail sweep side to side; the front stays stiff.

jellyfish.obj  bell centred on the origin, a typical 0.4-block one (they range 0.18 - 0.5).
  bell_top / bell_sides, rim_band (the bright edge), heart (the glowing core),
  thread_0..3 (long corner threads), arm_4..5 (the frilly middle arms)
  In game the bell squeezes in and stretches tall each beat, then eases wide and low; threads trail and sway.
  Everything on it glows and is see-through; the colour is picked per jellyfish, so the pink here is just a sample.

Send back .obj (+ .mtl) or .bbmodel files and I'll build them into the mod.
